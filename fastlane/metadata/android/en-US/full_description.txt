OpenKeyMOD turns your Android phone or tablet into a Bluetooth keyboard, mouse, trackpad, and macro pad for Windows, macOS, Linux, ChromeOS, Android, and Android TV.

It uses the standard Bluetooth HID protocol, so no companion app is needed on the target device. Pair once and control a computer or TV from your phone.

Features:

- Full keyboard, mouse, trackpad, media, and navigation controls.
- D-pad and TV-friendly remote layout.
- Shortcut Hub for reusable text snippets and macros.
- Compose & Send for quick text entry.
- Works with standard Bluetooth HID hosts.
- No internet permission, no ads, and no tracking.
- Open source under GPLv3.

Source code: https://github.com/sobanmujtaba/OpenKeyMOD
